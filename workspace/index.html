What goes into the numbers?

Gross revenue = available nights × occupancy × average daily rate. Net operating income subtracts variable and fixed expenses. Cash flow also subtracts loan payments. Cash on cash return divides annual cash flow by down payment, closing costs, and startup costs. Taxes, appreciation, sale costs, and principal paydown are excluded.
